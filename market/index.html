NEON
TYCOON

Trade across the city, build stalls and factories, play the stock market. 120 days to make your fortune.

How to play
  • Market: buy goods where they're cheap, travel, and sell where they're dear. Big trades move the price.
  • Travel or End day moves time on. Prices, news and your businesses update overnight.
  • Business: stalls sell goods for you every day; workshops make goods for 40% of their price. Put a workshop and a stall selling the same thing in one district and the workshop keeps the stall stocked.
  • Factories pay for production every day: keep some cash on hand or they stop.
  • More to manage: food and drink go off (old stock sells at half price), hire staff (👥) and keep them happy, order from suppliers (📦), watch the rival over the road, and sort out returns. Pinch to zoom, tap a shopper to see who they are.
  • In your shop: tap shoplifters (👀) before they reach the door, tap a raised hand 🙋 to help, drag a box from the stock pile onto a shelf, and open till 2 when the queue grows.
  • Run your shop: at one of your stalls, tap Open shop to serve the shoppers yourself. Set your price: too high and they walk out, too slow and they get tired of waiting.
  • Walk the floor: once Till 2 or a cashier is serving, tap 🚶 Walk and step out from behind the counter. Tap the floor to walk; tap a shopper to go to them. Near something, buttons appear: grab a box and stock a shelf, help, stop a shoplifter, pick up dropped packs, mop, wipe a window, or chat to a regular. 🛎️ Till takes you back.
  • Product lines: each shop sells two or three kinds of its good (a megastore three), one line per shelf. Shoppers want a particular one, and one line trends each day. Tap 🧱 Arrange shop to choose what goes on each shelf, move the islands and the floor displays. Islands near the door catch the eye (and shoplifters), baskets by the door and sweets by the till sell a little more.
  • Daily goal: every shop day has a 🎯 goal. Meet it for arcade coins, more for a streak. The closing report shows a heat map of where shoppers spent their time.
  • Your team and stockroom: tap 👥 to give each helper a job (till, restock, guard the door, break); tired helpers fumble. Deliveries come in the back door and are sorted into a stockroom bay per line; a red light means a line's run out.
  • Prices, promotions, orders: pick a line above the price to price it on its own. Buy a promo poster, 2-for-1 sign or tasting stand in 🧱 Arrange shop. Regulars sometimes ask you to have an order ready by a day: accept, and have it in when they come.
  • After closing: take the 🧹 night shift (30 s) to mop, face the shelves and empty the bin for a better rating. 📊 Report on the shop card shows your last seven shop days.
  • Word of mouth and running costs: what shoppers say about your prices, stock, queue and service brings more (or fewer) of them. Sign a 📜 supplier contract in Order stock for a locked price; rents are reviewed every 30 days; 10% tax on each week's profit. 📒 Cash flow is on the Bank tab.
  • 3D shop: your shop is built in 3D. Drag to turn it all the way round (360°), two fingers to slide, pinch to zoom; 📷 hides the buttons for a photo. A megastore opens an annex through the right-hand wall, with stairs up to Level 2. Prefer the drawn look? Pick 🖼️ Classic under 🎨 Decorate → Shop view.
  • Exchange: buy shares in Neon City companies; news moves them.
  • Bank: the loan costs 0.5% a day; savings earn 0.1% and are safe from pickpockets.
  • After 120 days your net worth sets your rank and pays arcade coins into your wallet.
