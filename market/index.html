NEON
TYCOON

Trade across the city, build stalls and factories, play the stock market. 120 days to make your fortune.

How to play
  • Market: buy goods where they're cheap, travel, and sell where they're dear. Big trades move the price.
  • Travel or End day moves time on. Prices, news and your businesses update overnight.
  • Business: stalls sell goods for you every day; workshops make goods for 40% of their price. Put a workshop and a stall selling the same thing in one district and the workshop keeps the stall stocked.
  • Factories pay for production every day: keep some cash on hand or they stop.
  • More to manage: food and drink go off (old stock sells at half price), hire staff (👥) and keep them happy, order from suppliers (📦), watch the rival over the road, and sort out returns. Pinch to zoom, tap a shopper to see who they are.
  • In your shop: tap shoplifters (👀) before they reach the door, tap a raised hand 🙋 to help, drag a box from the stock pile onto a shelf, and open till 2 when the queue grows.
  • Run your shop: at one of your stalls, tap Open shop to serve the shoppers yourself. Set your price: too high and they walk out, too slow and they get tired of waiting.
  • Exchange: buy shares in Neon City companies; news moves them.
  • Bank: the loan costs 0.5% a day; savings earn 0.1% and are safe from pickpockets.
  • After 120 days your net worth sets your rank and pays arcade coins into your wallet.
